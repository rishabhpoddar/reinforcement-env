Timeline of Fermentation

7000 BCE
🍶

Jiahu Brewery

Earliest known fermented drink — rice, honey, grape — found in Henan, China.

5500 BCE
🧀

First Cheese

Strainers recovered in Poland confirm cheesemaking in Neolithic Europe.

4000 BCE
🍺

Sumerian Beer

The Hymn to Ninkasi celebrates the goddess of beer in cuneiform.

200 BCE
🥬

Chinese Sauerkraut

Workers on the Great Wall fed fermented cabbage with rice wine.

700 CE
🌶

Early Kimchi

Korean records show salted vegetable preservation; chili added in 17th century.

1857
🔬

Pasteur Names Bacteria

Microbial responsibility for lactic fermentation identified. Microbes get a publicist.

2008
🧬

Microbiome Era

NIH’s Human Microbiome Project legitimizes the “gut feeling.”

2024
📓

Sauerkraut Census

8,212 home crocks reported — our largest community study to date.
