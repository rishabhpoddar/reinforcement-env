Membership

Members are the gravity that holds Polaris together. Two-thirds of our budget — and nearly all of our free K-12 programming — runs on membership dollars and the volunteer hours that come with them.

Stargazer
Individual
$85 / year

For the regular visitor who wants free general admission and 20% off everything.

  • Free general admission, year-round
  • 20% off events, courses, gift shop
  • Quarterly print newsletter, The Apogee
  • Member-only night, twice annually
  • Reciprocal admission at 41 ASTC observatories
Become a Stargazer
Astronomer
Household
$165 / year

Everything in Stargazer, for the whole household — plus priority booking and four guest passes.

  • All Stargazer benefits, for two adults + children
  • Four guest day-passes annually
  • Priority reservation on observing nights
  • Free admission to all public lectures
  • 10% off private dome bookings
  • Annual members' star party (October)
Become an Astronomer
Patron
Sustaining
$500 / year

For our most committed supporters. Your contribution funds free K-12 admission for a full school year.

  • All Astronomer benefits
  • Unlimited guest passes
  • Private 24" Cassegrain session, annually
  • Behind-the-scenes tour with the director
  • Recognition on the Donor Wall
  • Invitation to annual Patrons' Dinner
  • Tax-deductible above standard membership ($380)
