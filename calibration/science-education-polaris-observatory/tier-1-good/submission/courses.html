Courses & Workshops

Multi-session courses taught by working astronomers, photographers, and educators. Small cohorts, lots of telescope time, ongoing alumni community.

Beginner

Foundations of Naked-Eye Astronomy

6 sessions 2 hrs each Tuesdays, 7–9 PM Starts Jun 10

Learn to navigate the night sky without any equipment. By the end of six evenings, you'll identify thirty constellations, plot the ecliptic from memory, and understand the celestial coordinate system.

Dr. Renée Okafor
PhD Astronomy · Lecturer · 14 years teaching
$185 / full course

Full syllabus (6 sessions)
  • 01
    The celestial sphere & coordinate systems
    Right ascension, declination, and how the sky appears to rotate. Outdoor session under the dome.
  • 02
    Summer constellations & the milky way
    Identifying twelve summer constellations and the galactic plane overhead.
  • 03
    Planets & the ecliptic
    Tracking the wandering stars and predicting their positions.
  • 04
    Star colors & stellar classification
    Spectral types O through M, age and mass from color alone.
  • 05
    Meteor showers, comets & satellites
    Transient phenomena: what to watch for and when.
  • 06
    Final practical: navigation by stars
    A two-hour outdoor practical, finding latitude and time from the sky alone.

Dr. Annaliese Thornwood-Battista, PhD

Theoretical Cosmologist · Visiting Scholar 2026

Annaliese joined Polaris in 2024 after a decade at the Institute for Advanced Study. Her research focuses on the cosmic microwave background and constraints on inflationary models from next-generation experiments.

She teaches our Cosmology track and leads occasional public lectures on dark energy and the large-scale structure of the universe.

  • PhD, Theoretical Cosmology · Princeton, 2014
  • MSc, Mathematics · Cambridge, 2010
  • Royal Astronomical Society Fellow
  • Author, The Patient Universe (2022)
