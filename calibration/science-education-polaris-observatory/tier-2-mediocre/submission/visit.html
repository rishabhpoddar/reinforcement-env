Visit the Observatory

We're 47 miles east of Eugene, at 6,420 ft elevation along the western flank of the Cascades. The drive is half the experience — twilight in the canyon is something else.

Getting here

Directions & parking

22847 NF-19, Cascade Range, OR 97488
From Eugene: I-105 east to OR-126 east. After 38 miles, turn left on Forest Road 19 — climb 2,400 ft over nine winding miles. Allow 75 minutes from Eugene.

Free parking on-site for 80 vehicles. Overflow lot half a mile down (shuttle on busy nights). The last mile is unpaved gravel — passable in any vehicle but take it slow.

♿ Accessibility

Step-free access to planetarium dome and main visitor center. ADA-compliant restrooms, wheelchair-accessible viewing stations, and a portable handrail kit for outdoor patios.

  • Hearing loop in planetarium
  • Large-print sky guides on request
  • Service animals welcome

🚗 What to Bring

Nights at altitude run 20°F colder than the valley. Pack accordingly — even in August, sweaters are routine after midnight.

  • Warm layers, hat, gloves
  • Flashlight (red-light mode if available)
  • Bug repellent in summer
  • Folding chair for meteor watches

📷 Photography

Personal photography is welcome on the patio. Tripods permitted outside the dome. No flash, no white-light screens during observing hours — they ruin everyone's dark adaptation.

  • Astrophotography rigs welcome
  • Red-cellophane your phone screen
  • No drones (FAA restricted)

🍽️ Food & Drink

The Polaris Café serves coffee, hot cocoa, and light fare until 30 minutes before closing. Outside food is welcome on the picnic patios — leave no trace.

  • Hot drinks on observing nights
  • Vegan and gluten-free options
  • No alcohol on-site
For our shared darkness

Dark-Sky Etiquette

Our location averages Bortle 2 — among the darkest skies still accessible by paved road in Oregon. Help us keep it that way:

  • Use only red light flashlights past dusk
  • Park-and-stay; minimize headlight use after arrival
  • Dim or cover phone screens — even one bright screen affects all observers
  • Voices low after 10 PM; sound carries in the canyon
  • Pack out everything — including cigarette filters
  • Pets welcome on leash, but not in the planetarium
