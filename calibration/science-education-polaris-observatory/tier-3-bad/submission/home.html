Our mission

Sixty years of public stargazing in the high cascades.

Polaris Observatory was founded in 1962 by a group of astronomers and high-school teachers who believed the wonder of the night sky belonged to everyone. Today, we operate a research-grade 24-inch Cassegrain telescope, a 60-seat full-dome planetarium, and an outdoor observing patio with sixteen visitor scopes.

We are a non-profit, education-first institution. Roughly two-thirds of our annual programming is free to schools, families, and visitors in our region — funded by members, ticket sales, and the small army of volunteers who make every clear night possible.

— Dr. Vidya Saraswat, Director
