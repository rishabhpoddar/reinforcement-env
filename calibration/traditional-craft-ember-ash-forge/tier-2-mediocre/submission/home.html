The Smith

A craft kept alive by one steady fire.

I am Aldous Renn — third-generation blacksmith, son of a farrier, grandson of an Appalachian wheelwright. Ember & Ash is the working forge where I make my living, train apprentices, and teach the public what hot iron can teach a person about patience.

Every commission that leaves this shop carries a small punched anvil-mark. It is my promise that the piece was made — start to finish — by hand, in this building, with intention.

— Aldous Renn, Master Smith
