Walnut Hill Estate Gate

A 9-foot-wide pair of estate gates hand-forged from reclaimed wrought iron and mild steel. Inspired by the orchard the property is known for, the upper panels feature hand-punched leaf forms and forge-welded scrollwork. Installed Spring 2026 in Rhinebeck, NY.
