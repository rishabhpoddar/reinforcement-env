From Brief to Doorstep

Six steps. One steady fire.

Commissions usually run 4 – 16 weeks depending on scale. You will hear from me at every stage, and you are welcome at the forge to see the work in progress.

01

Consultation

An hour in person or by call to understand the piece, its setting, and the life you want it to live.

02

Sketches

Hand drawings to scale. Two or three iterations until the line and proportion feel right.

03

Material

Sourcing the right stock — reclaimed wrought iron, fresh mild steel, high-carbon bar.

04

Forging

The longest stage. Heat, hammer, repeat — sometimes for weeks. Updates and photos along the way.

05

Finishing

Wire-brushing, waxing, heat-bluing, oiling — whatever the piece asks for, applied by hand.

06

Delivery

Local installation by hand. Long-distance shipping crated by the same hands that forged it.

Investment

Pricing guidance.

Every commission is bid individually. The brackets below give an honest sense of what most projects land within once design and material are finalized.

Tier I

Small Hardware

$120 – $850

Hooks, brackets, handles, fire tools, door knockers, kitchenware.

  • 1 – 3 week turnaround
  • 1 hammered finish included
  • Hand-punched anvil-mark
Tier II · Most Common

Mid-Scale Fixtures

$900 – $6,400

Sconces, chandeliers, andirons, sculptural objects, single-panel railings.

  • 4 – 8 week turnaround
  • Two finish options
  • Installation consult included
Tier III

Architectural

$6,500+

Estate gates, full railing systems, public sculpture, ecclesiastical work.

  • 10 – 20 week turnaround
  • Full design package
  • On-site installation
