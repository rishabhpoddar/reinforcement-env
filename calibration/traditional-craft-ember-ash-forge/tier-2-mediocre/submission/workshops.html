What to Expect

Everything is provided, except your nerve.

All workshops include safety briefing, all equipment, materials, lunch, and the steel you carry home at the end.

Apron Provided

Heavy leather apron, gloves, and safety glasses fitted on arrival. Bring closed-toe shoes and cotton clothes.

Safety First

Full briefing before any flame. Every workstation is supervised, with a fire watch on duty all session.

Lunch Provided

Hot lunch from the kitchen next door — soup, bread, something braised. Dietary needs accommodated with notice.

Take Home Your Work

Whatever you forge is yours. We'll wrap it in oiled paper and tie it with twine when the steel is cool.

Your Instructors

Two smiths. One bellows.

All workshops are co-taught — one smith at the anvil, one circulating the benches.

Aldous Renn

Master Smith · Founder

Third-generation blacksmith. Trained in the Appalachian wheelwright tradition before opening Ember & Ash in 2009. Specializes in architectural and Damascus work. Has taught over 1,400 students.

Marisol Vance

Resident Smith · Bladesmithing Lead

ABS-trained bladesmith, formerly of the Pittsburgh Iron Cooperative. Joined the forge in 2019. Leads our knife and Damascus intensives and runs the heat-treat oven.
