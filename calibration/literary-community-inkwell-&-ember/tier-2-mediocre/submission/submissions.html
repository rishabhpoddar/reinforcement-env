Editorial guidelines

What we want and what we don't

What we want

Speculative fiction with atmospheric texture, memorable images, and a strong emotional center.

Poems that earn their turns. Essays that interrogate the strange with intelligence and warmth.

What we do not want

Cliched horror beats, thin worldbuilding, or work that mistakes opacity for depth.

Please avoid simultaneous submissions unless noted in the cover letter.

Payment rates

Short fiction: 0.20/word. Poetry: $60 per poem. Essays: $150 flat.

We pay on acceptance and confirm within the response window listed below.

Response times

Fiction and essays: 4 to 8 weeks. Poetry: 2 to 4 weeks. We reply to every submission.

Status updates are sent to the address used in the form.
